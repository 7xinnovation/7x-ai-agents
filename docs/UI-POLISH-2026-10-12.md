# UI polish shared by both agents — 12 October 2026

Six small changes to the widget every tenant renders (Emirates Post's Mira and
EPGL alike). Found by walking both agents in Chrome at desktop and at phone
width, in English and Arabic. Code only; nothing to apply per environment.

1. **The header fits a phone.** At 390px the wordmark ran under the language
   chip, "Sign in" wrapped onto two lines and six controls fought for one row.
   Below 600px the mark shrinks first, chips tighten to 40px, the language
   toggle keeps its word and drops its globe, and chip labels never wrap.
2. **The application panel reads as words.** A value recorded under a key no
   journey labels showed the key itself ("contact_name"); enumerations showed
   their codes ("1_YEAR", "gateway", "DXB", "true"). Field options now travel
   to the widget, so a value shows its option label ("1 year", "Pay with Card",
   "Dubai"); a key with no label is humanised ("Contact name"); booleans read
   Yes/No in the session language; short codes without a label stay as they
   are. `app/embed/[agent]/humanize.ts`, used for the details and the
   requirements list.
3. **Reduced motion means still.** Three animations honoured the preference;
   eleven did not. One rule under the widget root now collapses every
   animation and transition.
4. **One focus ring.** Keyboard and switch users get a 2px ring in the agent's
   colour on every control that had none; controls with their own keep it.
5. **One press.** Every enabled button moves 1px on press; those with their own
   press keep it.
6. Nothing changed in copy, colour, radius or layout above phone width.

Checked: `scripts/test-ui-polish-2026-10-12.ts` (12), plus the suites that pin
the touched lines (feedback 29 September 177, EPGL consolidated 26, consent
time 40, feedback 12 October 33, the three sign-out suites), tsc clean.

Not done, on purpose: the greeting copy of both agents still carries an em
dash; it is reviewed client copy, not chrome. The widget has no dark theme
because every host is light.
